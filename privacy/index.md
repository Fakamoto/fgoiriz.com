# Privacy information

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This page explains how fgoiriz.com handles information. The site is the static personal website of Facundo Goiriz. It publishes a professional profile, a CV, contact details, and machine-readable files. It does not provide user accounts, a contact form, comments, purchases, or other features that ask visitors to submit personal information on the site.

## Hosting and delivery

The site is hosted with GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. When you request a page or file, these infrastructure providers may process standard network and request data, such as your IP address, browser or user-agent information, the requested path, and request time. They use this data to deliver the site, maintain service reliability, and protect the service. Their handling of that data is governed by their own terms and privacy notices.

## Data collected by this site

The published site code does not include an analytics service, advertising tracker, account system, database, or form submission endpoint. The site itself does not intentionally set cookies. Infrastructure providers may still keep operational or security logs as part of delivering the site. Facundo does not receive personal information from this site unless you choose to send an email or contact him through an external service.

## Email and external links

Selecting the email link opens your email application. If you send a message to [facundogoiriz@gmail.com](mailto:facundogoiriz@gmail.com), your email provider and Google process the message, and Facundo receives the information you include so he can read and answer it. The site also links to GitHub, LinkedIn, Konecta Labs, and other external websites. Those sites control their own data practices. Review their privacy information before sending data to them.

## Privacy questions

For a question about this site or a message you sent directly to Facundo, email [facundogoiriz@gmail.com](mailto:facundogoiriz@gmail.com). Include enough detail to identify the message or issue. Do not send passwords, private keys, financial details, medical information, or other sensitive data through ordinary email.
